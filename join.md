## How to apply

1. **PhD and M.Tech/MS (Research):** apply through the regular IIT Jodhpur admission call, and mention the MMIS Lab and the project you are interested in.
2. **Before applying, or for internships:** email <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a> with the subject line **"MMIS Lab — [position]"**, attaching your CV and a short note (half a page) on why the project interests you and any relevant experience.

We read every email, but may only be able to reply to candidates whose background matches an open position.
