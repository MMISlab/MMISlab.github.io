We work on PET and SPECT imaging, combining physics, simulation and AI to make molecular imaging more accurate, more quantitative and lower-dose. Our aim is to turn imaging into dependable measurements that support diagnosis, treatment planning and therapy. The work is done with clinical and industry partners.

## Focus areas

- Physics-based modelling and Monte Carlo simulation of PET and SPECT systems
- AI methods for image reconstruction, denoising and low-dose imaging
- Quantitative imaging for diagnosis, treatment planning and therapy monitoring
- Task-based image quality and system performance evaluation
- Translation and validation with clinical and industry partners

## Why it matters

More reliable quantification, lower radiation dose for patients, and images that clinicians can use as a measurement rather than only a picture.
