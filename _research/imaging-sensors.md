We are developing a compact, low-dose intraoral X-ray imaging sensor that gives dentists richer diagnostic information. The project runs from sensor development through to a clinical pilot with a partner dental hospital.

## Focus areas

- Sensor characterisation
- Prototype engineering
- Validation on teeth and jaw models
- Clinical pilot study with a partner dental hospital

## Why it matters

Earlier detection of caries, better assessment of bone and implants, and lower radiation dose for patients.
