The Multimodal Medical Imaging Systems (MMIS) Lab has started at the Department of Bioscience and Bioengineering, IIT Jodhpur. The lab works on PET, SPECT and X-ray imaging systems, combining physics, simulation and AI to make medical imaging more accurate, quantitative and lower-dose.
