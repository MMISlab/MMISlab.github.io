Our approach

We work across the whole imaging chain: detector and system physics, Monte Carlo simulation, image reconstruction, AI and task-based image-quality evaluation, through to clinical validation. Projects are carried out with clinical and industry partners so that methods are tested where they will be used.

  • PhysicsDetectors, system modelling and performance
  • SimulationMonte Carlo and digital models of real scanners
  • AIReconstruction, denoising and low-dose imaging
  • ClinicQuantification and validation with partners
